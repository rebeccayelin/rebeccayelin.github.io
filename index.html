I design computational systems where mathematics and materials become forms for thinking, making, and playing.

I'm an EECS PhD candidate, advised by Erik Demaine in CSAIL. I've designed systems for garment making at Roblox and Adobe, and explored code as a creative medium at the Media Lab.

My time at MIT is a collage of research, teaching, and art-making.
I also collect prose, poems, postcards, and so many plants.138.5 and counting. My first cutting from Polina is now 13 feet long.

Systems

My work reimagines material systems as computational playgrounds.

Refashion: Reconfigurable Garments via Modular Design

UIST 2025 · Rebecca Lin, Michal Lukáč, Mackenzie Leake

How can we design garments for change and reuse? By treating garments as dynamic assemblies rather than static products, Refashion enables users to remix their garments on demand.2Refashion was born on Aug. 15, 2024, when I raided the Adobe office for 600 Post-it notes and stayed up all night making a ballgown.

Project Page· MIT News· MIT MAD

Graph Threading

ITCS 2024 · (a-b) Erik D. Demaine, Yael Kirkpatrick, Rebecca Lin

How can we thread tubes with a single string to achieve a target connectivity when the string is pulled taut? By casting "threadings" as constrained walks on graphs, we give efficient algorithms for computing minimum-length threadings.3Zhecheng sent me a video of a deployable bamboo sculpture by the wonderful Alison Martin, and the rest was history.

X Thread· arXiv

Encoding-Decoding Constellations

Rebecca Lin, Craig S. Kaplan

How can we create constellation patterns with unconventional star arrangements? By representing constellations as mathematical graphs, we reveal an alternative language for reasoning about and creating constellations.
